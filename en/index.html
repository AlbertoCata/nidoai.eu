Ovum, Nido’s engine

25 GB of model.
8 GB of memory.

Nido’s model takes up about 25 GB, and the iPhone 16 Pro Max has 8 GB of memory. Ovum, Nido’s inference engine, keeps only what’s essential in memory and reads the rest from storage as it writes.

Measured in the app itself, on an iPhone 16 Pro Max. A token is the chunk of text the model works with: about 3.6 characters of Spanish.

Fourteen in-house techniques.

Ovum starts from colibri, an open-source engine that runs huge mixture-of-experts models on computers. To bring it to a phone, all of the GPU code and about 40% of the model code are new.

  • Learning cache
  • Mixed 4- and 8-bit trunk
  • qs16 scales
  • Cache-aware routing
  • Layer lookahead
  • Expert predictorin research
  • Custom Metal executor
  • Layer-by-layer GPU reads
  • Warm start
  • Sampling without sorting the vocabulary
  • WFE-based waiting
  • Thermal governorin testing
  • Lightweight UI while answering
  • An interface that tells the truth

Frequently asked questions

What people ask most.

Does Nido work without an internet connection?

Yes. The AI model lives on the iPhone, so Nido answers just the same in airplane mode, on the subway or wherever there’s no signal.

Do my conversations leave my iPhone?

No. They’re stored only on your iPhone, and Nido needs no account and no server to answer. Nobody else reads them or uses them to train models.

Which AI model does Nido use?

Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, a 35-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model from Alibaba Cloud’s Qwen team. It runs on Ovum, Nido’s inference engine.

How does a 25 GB model fit on an iPhone with 8 GB of memory?

Ovum keeps the model’s most-used experts in memory and reads the rest from storage as it writes. When they’re needed, 92–93% of them are already in memory.

Is it fast?

On an iPhone 16 Pro Max, the first word of a new chat arrives in 1.6 seconds, and Nido writes at about 11 tokens per second with the phone cool (about 7 on average in long answers, once the iPhone warms up).

How much does it cost?

Nido is free, with unlimited chat. Nido Pro costs €25.99 as a one-time purchase, with no subscription, and works with Family Sharing.

When is it coming out?

Nido is coming soon to the App Store. Chat already works in the app; the other features are designed and in development.

For investors

From 3.4 to 11 tokens per second in five days.

Every Ovum improvement is measured on the same iPhone, in the app itself and with the phone cool. When cool, Nido already beats the goal of 10 tokens per second; the work now is to make heat cost it less speed in long answers.

€0in server costs per answer

The iPhone does the computing.

A cloud AI service pays for the servers behind every answer it gives. With Nido, the user’s own iPhone computes it. That’s why Nido Pro can be sold for a one-time price, and the product keeps working even if cloud prices change.
